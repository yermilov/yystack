# Council brief template — used by SKILL.md Phase 4

Use this exact template for `<output>/council/<slug>/brief.md`, then run the pre-handoff check in SKILL.md Phase 4.

```markdown
# [Topic] — Council Brief

**Question:** [the framed question from phase 0]
**Council:** [each member + its model and mode]; **Depth:** [Quick / Standard / Deep Research]; **Rounds:** [1, 2 or 3 — Stage 1 counts as round 1]
**Date:** [YYYY-MM-DD]

## tl;dr

[3–5 sentences. The actual answer if there is one, or the actual disagreement
if there isn't. The merged recommendation if cross-pollination produced one.]

## What the council agreed on (after deliberation)

[Bullets or short paragraphs. Each substantive claim should be specific enough
that someone could verify or disprove it.]

## Where the council disagreed — and what they converged on

[For each round-1 disagreement: what each side initially claimed, the strongest
reasoning for each, whether round 2 produced convergence and on what terms,
my read on the merged recommendation.]

## What I (Chairman) checked or pushed back on

[Citations I verified with my own web search. Claims I think one or both models got
wrong despite their confidence. Claims that smell like hallucinations and I
couldn't confirm.]

## Recommendation

[If the question has a decision attached, my recommendation and confidence.
If it's a learning topic, the 1–3 things the reader should walk away with.]

## Open questions / what's still unclear

[Things the council couldn't settle. Suggest the next move if it matters.]

## Sources

[Real URLs the council cited that I verified. Drop hallucinated sources
entirely — better empty than wrong.]
```
